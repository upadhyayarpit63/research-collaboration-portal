Project Overview

A centralized platform for student research teams to collaborate, track milestones, manage tasks, and share references efficiently in real time.

Key Objectives

  • Task Tracking: Monitor daily progress and assign responsibilities transparently.
  • Resource Management: Maintain a centralized repository of research papers, datasets, and media files.
